- Trust is not a slogan
- It comes from knowing who is accountable, how the system is evaluated, where the safeguards site, and when a human must make the final call

# Evolution of AI

We went from:

1. PCs = Computing power in the hands of everyday workers
2. Internet = Information, communication, and connectivity in office buildings around the world
3. Mobile = Computing, communication, and connectivity anywhere in the world
4. Cloud = Scalable computing, elasticity, OpEx flexibility on demand, leaner workforces
5. AI = Knowledge and expertise

The first 4 were "technology solutions" - they allowed "technologists" to leverage them to create new efficiencies, markets. AI:
- Aemocratizes technology and makes knowledge more immediately available
- Lowers the cost of expertise around technical solutions, which transforms every industry and profession

### The Evolution of Banking

1. 1920s-1950s
    - Physical buildings with local safes, armed guards
    - Handwritten ledgers / double accounting practices
2. 1960s-1980s
    - Physical buildings in center of town with branches
    - Mainframe storage with tape backups
    - 1985: a large US bank had 10-50GB worth of storage
3. 1990s-2022
    - Internet-enabled banking
    - Customer Service call centers
    - Large banks managed own data centers
4. 2022+
    - Consumer-facing AI agents
    - Data explosion with banks having 100PB of data to manage
    - Data analytics is king - data lakes, warehouses, unstructured, but still heavily siloed

# Phases of AI Growth

## Phase 1: Humans + AI Assistants

Every employee has a Copilot-like tool available. 

## Phase 2: Humans + AI Agents

Agents join teams as "digital colleagues", taking on specific tasks at human direction. Examples:
- Review alerts proactively and escalate "true positives"
- Identify anomalies in metrics proactively

## Phase 3: Humans + Teams + Workflows of AI Agents

Humans set direction and agents run entire business processes and workflows, checking in as needed.

- Mission Control = your single pane of glass to view all agents, teams, results
- Sentinel = anticipate and mitigate risks. Score vulnerabilities, recommend clear actions to protect workloads
- Decision Hub = where the teams collaborate and respond to AI Agents approval needs
